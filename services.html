Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply click "Book" next to any service or use the "Book on WhatsApp" button. A formatted message with your service request will be created for you on WhatsApp. You can also call us directly at +91 83539 09431.

Do you provide freelance bridal makeup at venues outside the salon?

Yes! Jeba Siddiqui and her team are available for on-location bridal and party makeup across Lucknow (hotels, banquet halls, or private residences). Please contact us in advance to check date availability.

What makeup brands do you use?

We use 100% genuine global cosmetics including MAC Cosmetics, Huda Beauty, NARS, Kryolan, Laura Mercier, and Anastasia Beverly Hills.

Where is Zeva Makeup Studio located?

We are situated at Amrapali Yojana, Dubagga, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. It is a quiet, comfortable, women-only studio.
